Where each part comes from

Every shape and parameter count comes from one hooked forward pass of the code's own VAE and Denoiser classes (toy_caption_dial_ldm.py, lines 69 to 99). Saved samples come untouched from the npz files in artifacts/results/how-much-co-occurrence-the-product-needs/, and their counts from the matching json files.

The latent, the denoiser's inputs and output, the caption table and the 200-step trajectories come from one model this page's build script retrained on CPU, with the same code and seed as run 1 at 2,000 relabelled pictures. It is not the saved run, because CPU and GPU arithmetic differ, so its samples differ from the saved ones.

SDXL's sizes are read from the stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0 configs on this laptop (text encoders of 768 and 1,280 numbers per token, 77 token slots, cross-attention width 2,048), and its tokens from its own tokenizers. The encoder names CLIP ViT-L and OpenCLIP ViT-bigG come from the SDXL paper, not the config.

How to read the page

Each screen does one job. Back, Next, the dots or the arrow keys move between screens, and the chapter buttons jump. Tags say what a picture is: real means computed from the code or saved by an experiment, config means read from SDXL's files, and illustrative means drawn to explain and not measured. Red and blue always mean the red circle and the blue square. Purple means a blob where the two colours mixed. Teal marks the mechanism being explained.

What is not drawn

  • The VAE's training loss (reconstruction error plus a KL term weighted 0.0001) and its restart rule (up to 5 tries until error is at most 0.012). Named on the VAE screen's notes, not drawn.
  • The colour scorer that counts "separate red circle and blue square". Its rule is in the result screen's notes.
  • The single-caption and joint-caption samples for every model. They are saved and pass their checks; only PoE samples are shown.
  • Models 1, 2 and 4 of the write-up. This page is model 3 only.
  • The SDXL UNet's inside. Its side of the caption screen stops at the text encoders' outputs.
  • The latent of each saved sample. Samples are shown decoded, as pictures.
