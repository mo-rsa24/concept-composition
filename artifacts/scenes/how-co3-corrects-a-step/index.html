where these numbers come from

Illustrative numbers, released formulas

The vectors are invented. The latent and the four noise predictions have two entries each so they can be drawn. The released 1024 × 1024 configuration uses a latent with 4 × 128 × 128 = 65,536 entries.

The predictions stay fixed. Moving a slider recomputes the arithmetic on the same four vectors. A real U-Net would return new predictions whenever x or the timestep changes.

The arithmetic check. co3_corrector from composers/utils/attn_utils.py at commit 35ea972 ran on CPU with a stub U-Net and a small NumPy stand-in for the tensor calls. The page's JavaScript reached the same final x on the 2 settings shown here and 400 random inputs, to within 0.00001. This checks the toy's arithmetic. It does not test SDXL predictions or GPU mixed-precision execution.

The gap belongs to this toy. Comparing largest entries over two values does not predict the gap on a 65,536-entry latent. Real distances dk were not measured, and they set the size of the weights.

When the largest-entry rescale misbehaves. Its denominator is the sum's largest signed entry. At 0 it is undefined, near 0 the scale factor can grow without limit, and opposite signs in its numerator and denominator reverse the sum's direction. The later norm match fixes the length, and it too is undefined if the re-noised vector is zero.

What the two paths are. Both use the slider settings and the same fixed predictions. The Algorithm 3 path applies the printed rescale and stops after line 7. Neither path runs a full sampler. The switch-off press opens at guidance 5.0 and β 0.8 and removes the code's two rescales together, while Algorithm 3 keeps its own.

One thing the page leaves open. Which guidance setting a reproduction should use is board question 16, and the owner has not decided. This page takes no side.

Sources. The paper is “Steer Away From Mode Collisions”: Algorithms 2 and 3, Eqs. 4 and 12 to 15, Proposition 1, section 4.1, and appendices A.4 and A.8. The released script is run_sample_co3.sh.
