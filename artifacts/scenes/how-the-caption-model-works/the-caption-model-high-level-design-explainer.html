Read it top to bottom. Pictures with caption ids train a VAE that squeezes each picture into 128 numbers, a denoiser learns to clean noisy versions of those 128 numbers given a caption id, and PoE sampling calls that one denoiser three times per step to draw a picture for two captions at once.

Where each box comes from, and what is not drawn

Where each box comes from

All paths are under artifacts/notes/what-poe-loses-when-it-composes/.

The three piles of pictures: toy_caption_dial_ldm.py:44-48 (single circles and squares at x = 6, 16 or 26; the two both-shapes layouts) and toy_sampler_hybrids.py:38-62 (16 x 32 pixels, red RGB 0.85, 0.18, 0.15, blue RGB 0.16, 0.35, 0.80). 2,000 of each: IMAGES // 3 with IMAGES = 6000, toy_caption_dial_ldm.py:39.

Relabel k pictures: corpus_k, toy_caption_threshold.py:30-42. Exactly k of the 2,000 both-shapes pictures are added twice more, under id 0 and under id 1. The counts run were 0, 1, 5, 20, 50, 200, 600, 1,200 and 2,000, each trained three times (seeds 20261022 to 20261024).

Training set: pixel noise of sd 0.1 (SIGMA0, toy_sampler_hybrids.py:39) added at toy_caption_threshold.py:40.

VAE: class VAE, toy_caption_dial_ldm.py:69-86. Encoder 3 to 32 to 64 to 8 channels, the 8 split into a mean and a log-variance of 4 each on a 4 x 8 grid. Parameters counted from the layer shapes: encoder 24,008, decoder 21,699, total 45,707. Trained by train_vae, lines 102-128: 4,000 steps, Adam at 0.001 with a cosine schedule, restarted on a new seed up to 5 times until reconstruction error is at most 0.012.

Add noise at level k and Squared error: train_denoiser, toy_caption_dial_ldm.py:144-157. The loss compares the output with the clean latent, so the denoiser predicts the clean latent, never the noise. 8,000 steps, batch 256, Adam at 0.0002. Cosine schedule over 200 levels: toy_sampler_hybrids.py:40, 66-70.

Caption id, empty 10% of the time: toy_caption_dial_ldm.py:152; id 3 is the empty caption (line 42).

Caption vector: nn.Embedding(4, 64), toy_caption_dial_ldm.py:92, a learned table with one row of 64 numbers per caption id. No text is read anywhere in the model.

Denoiser: class Denoiser, toy_caption_dial_ldm.py:89-99. Input is 128 noisy latent numbers, 32 sines and 32 cosines of the level k, and the 64-number caption vector, 256 in all; three hidden layers of 1,024 with SiLU; 128 out. 2,493,568 parameters in the layers plus 256 in the caption table = 2,493,824.

Sampling with PoE: draw, toy_caption_dial_ldm.py:161-178. Each step turns each of the three clean-latent estimates into a noise estimate, e = (z − sqrt(abar) x estimate) / sqrt(1 − abar), sums guidance 3 over the two single captions, and takes one ancestral step. Guidance 3 is GUIDE, line 40.

Langevin corrector: toy_caption_corrector.py:37-63. After each step at level j: z moves by eta x s plus noise of size sqrt(2 eta), with s = −e / sqrt(1 − abar_j) and eta = 0.05 (1 − abar_j). 5 moves is the judged setting, 20 the stronger one.

Count the shapes: toy_blob_scorer.py:1-73. A sample is a separate red circle and blue square when it has at least 12 red and 12 blue pixels, centroids at least 6 pixels apart along x, and fewer than 12 purple pixels.

SDXL comparison: SDXL's UNet has about 2.6 billion parameters and reads its prompt through two CLIP text encoders (arXiv 2307.01952, section 2.1). This model's denoiser is about 1,000 times smaller and reads a caption as a row of a 4-row table.

How to read the lines

Solid: data the next part waits on. Dotted with two heads under the reverse step: the optional Langevin corrector, which takes the latent and hands it back. The denoiser in lane 4 is the one trained in lane 3, called three times per step.

Lanes 1 to 3 are training; lane 4 is drawing a new picture once training is done. One full training of the VAE and the denoiser takes about 25 seconds on a laptop GPU.

What is not drawn

The one-caption sampler (guidance 3 on a single caption, e = e_empty + 3 (e_caption − e_empty)) is the same loop with two denoiser calls; it is described here, not drawn.

The template-library scorer in toy_sampler_hybrids.py is computed by draw but the write-up's counts use the colour scorer, so only the colour scorer is drawn.

The earlier relabelling by a random share (corpus, the dial 0 to 1) is replaced in the write-up by exact counts k, so only the exact-count relabelling is drawn.

The VAE's KL term (weight 0.0001) and the log-variance clamp are inside the VAE box and not drawn as parts.

No pictures sit behind the boxes in this version; the write-up's sample grids are linked from its own folder.
