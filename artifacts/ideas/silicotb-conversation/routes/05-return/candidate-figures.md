# candidate-figures.md

Cancer masked by tuberculosis is documented on **plain chest radiographs**, so the fourth way is not confined to CT in the literature searched. [Kim et al., 2001, *Korean Journal of Radiology*, Results and Figs. 1, 4](https://www.kjronline.org/DOIx.php?id=10.3348/kjr.2001.2.3.138)

**Our annotation convention:** 1 = separate places; 2 = shared region with distinguishable components; 3 = merged appearance; 4 = present but obscured.

**Our evidence convention:** “inferred” identifies our proposed mapping from the authors’ words, while “candidate” means that part of the requested definition remains unproved.

**Our counting convention:** rows identify figure selections rather than independent patients, and different modalities from one published figure are explicitly identified.

Quoted wording below consists of short caption excerpts unless labelled **prose**; “marks unconfirmed” means that the caption does not specify arrows or other marks and image retrieval did not establish their presence.

**Our highest-priority selections for annotation are rows 01–07:** they combine explicit masking evidence or plausible merged appearances with verified licences allowing reuse, subject to their stated conditions.

The thick-walled-cyst candidates have supporting comparative pathology: Inomata and colleagues observed these lesions in 16/22 CPFE patients, versus 0/8 with IPF alone and 0/17 with emphysema alone. [Inomata et al., 2014, *BMC Pulmonary Medicine*, Table 3 and “Clinicoradiological assessment”](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-2466-14-104)

**Our interpretation:** that comparison supports prioritising a combination-specific morphology, but does not establish the stronger proposition that neither disease remains nameable from the image.

**Our search finding:** no verified chest-radiograph caption was found that establishes the strict third way, despite searches for pneumonia–emphysema pseudocavitation/pseudohoneycombing, combined fibrosis–emphysema cysts, and tuberculosis–cancer composite lesions.

**Our reuse rule:** the 20 rows carrying verified CC BY or CC BY-NC licences are the reuse shortlist, with NC rows restricted to noncommercial use and all five uncertain-licence rows retained as look-only.

The Cottin manuscript’s institutional repository directs readers to publisher terms rather than supplying an independently sufficient figure-reuse permission. [UCL Discovery, Cottin et al. record, “Additional information”](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10155484/)

Kim’s Fig. 2 was deliberately excluded from the reuse shortlist because its caption credits an earlier publication and states that it was reproduced with permission. [Kim et al., 2001, *Korean Journal of Radiology*, Fig. 2 credit](https://www.kjronline.org/DOIx.php?id=10.3348/kjr.2001.2.3.138)

**Our limitation:** this is a caption-and-rights candidate inventory, not a completed pixel audit, and entries marked “marks unconfirmed” still require inspection of the original downloadable image before annotation.
