# Reference images fetched during the claim walk

Each image is here because a claim in the conversation asserts something about what the combined
disease looks like, and the transcript carries no radiograph of its own. Licence is stated per
image; nothing here is reused without one.

## silicotuberculosis-pa-radiograph-cureus-2024.png

**What it shows.** A posteroanterior chest radiograph of a 40-year-old stone cutter with twenty
years of silica exposure and bronchoscopically confirmed tuberculosis. Diffuse small rounded
opacities across the upper and middle zones, coalescence into progressive massive fibrosis in the
upper lobes with volume loss, enlarged hila, and linear fibrotic opacities at the apices. The
report calls that whole pattern "classical for silicosis with superimposed TB", then says a
definitive call still needs the occupational history and the microbiology.

**Where it came from.** Figure 1 of Elango R, Kailash A, Verma G, Rudhramoorty P. Silico-
Tuberculosis Mimicking Malignancy. *Cureus* 16(7): e65411, 26 July 2024.
DOI 10.7759/cureus.65411. Extracted from the publisher's own PDF at
`assets.cureus.com/uploads/case_report/pdf/269270/`.

**Licence.** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), stated on the article's first page:
unrestricted use, distribution and reproduction in any medium provided the original author and
source are credited. Reusable in the manuscript with that credit line.

**Which claims it serves.** 1.1 (whether the order of onset changes the appearance), 1.2 (the name
of the combined condition), 1.3 (what the posteroanterior film shows), 2.8 (mimicry), 2.11 (the
combined condition needs no new radiological feature).
