## Why this reading

[↑ top](#table-of-contents)

The question read across all four files: how two chest diseases sit relative to each other in one
chest, and which of those arrangements anyone has published a picture of. Step 2 reads this map to
decide what the paper claims, and decisions 1 and 4 of the scoped problem rest on it.

The four files are one literature search run on 2026-11-04 over sixteen disease pairs. They are a
report that cites papers. A claim in them is only as good as its citation, and a claim with none is
marked the report's own inference. The plain readings and comparisons in this map are this walk's
reading of the quoted text. Patient data lives on the cluster and nothing here touches it.

**Settled before the walk** (from the [silicotuberculosis idea map](../../ideas/silicotb-conversation/IDEA_MAP.md) and the return files):

- separate places, measured on our own VinDr-CXR films: aortic enlargement with cardiomegaly on 1,951 of 1,972 films carrying both (98%), and the share does not move at any box-overlap floor
- same region, measured: lung opacity with pulmonary fibrosis on 201 of 489 films (41%) at an intersection-over-union floor of 0.25, median 0.405
- merged and present-but-unreadable cannot be read off annotation boxes at all
- the report found 7 candidates for merged and verified none, and no chest radiograph caption establishing it
- the report proposes a fifth state, both diseases confirmed and neither visible, from 4 of 54 normal-radiograph patients carrying two simultaneous infections
- the pair with a quantitative comparison is pulmonary fibrosis with emphysema: a lesion specific to the combination in 16 of 22 patients, against 0 of 8 with fibrosis alone and 0 of 17 with emphysema alone

## The four arrangements, pair by pair

Each table sorts the 16 pairs in [pairs and their regimes](../../ideas/silicotb-conversation/routes/05-return/pairs-and-their-regimes.md)
by how the report scored that arrangement. "Documented" means a caption or study the report quotes
establishes it. "Inferred or candidate" means the report mapped it from prose or found it short of a
defining condition. "Absent" means the report marked it not documented, which says not found in the
sources examined, not proved absent. A last row holds pairs outside the 16 that the other three
files bring in.

## The fifth state

[↑ top](#table-of-contents)

Both diseases established without the image, and the image shows neither. Source: Yoo 2011, 4 of 54
HIV-seropositive patients with normal radiographs had two simultaneous infections, prose only, no
patient film ([where the four regimes fail, line 9](../../ideas/silicotb-conversation/routes/05-return/where-the-four-regimes-fail.md#L9)).
The report's own boundary: if the framework covers only already abnormal regions, this is an
exclusion, not a fifth arrangement (line 28).
