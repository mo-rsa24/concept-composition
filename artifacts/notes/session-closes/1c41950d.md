## What it set out to do

This session ran inside `2-classifier-guidance/` from 2026-11-17 08:43 to 2026-11-19 02:56 UTC. It asked whether a small classifier's gradient can steer SDXL's product of experts so that one starting noise ends as a separate, clean cat and dog. The user ruled out drawing many candidates and picking the best: one seed must follow one trajectory to the pair.

It began as [plan 21](../../../2-classifier-guidance/plans/21-rung2-a-small-discriminator-steers-cat-x-dog.md), rung 2 of [the classifier-guided route](../../../2-classifier-guidance/plans/route-classifier-guided-poe-from-toy-to-animals.md). It grew into plans 22 to 31 and an unattended improvement loop.

**Words this uses**

- **PoE (product of experts):** `ε_null + 7.5 · (d_cat + d_dog)`, where each `d` is that prompt's noise prediction minus the empty prompt's.
- **Checker and push:** the checker is a classifier scoring a half-drawn picture. The push adds its gradient to PoE's noise prediction.
- **Cap:** the largest push allowed at a step, as a fraction of the size of PoE's prediction.
- **Readout checker:** a small trained head reading the features of a frozen SDXL.
- **The kept recipe:** the readout checker trained across 243 pairs, at strength 30 and cap 0.4 on steps 0 to 15 of 50, then plain PoE.
- **Scorer:** `score_sheet.py`, a Claude API labeller. Its "faithful pair" means two bodies, one clear of each concept, no borrowed feature, an intact photograph.
- **Eye calls:** Claude's own calls from the pictures. **Clean** is a clear cat beside a clear dog. **Near-clean** allows one small flaw. The user has not confirmed these calls.
- **Random push:** a control with the same size as the real push and a random direction.

### The CO3 ideas it proposed

The session read CO3's code to answer "is there anything from CO3 we could use?". Over the first 6 of 50 steps, [CO3's corrector](../../../composition/debottam_co3/composers/utils/attn_utils.py) moves the latent using the joint prompt and the two single-concept predictions. The settings below come from [the launcher this project uses](../../../1-lora-correction/scripts/showcase/run_co3_targets.sh).

**A. Up-weight the concept that is losing (the session's first pick, unanswered).** The proposal replaces PoE's plain sum with weights that favour the swallowed concept, and needs no training:

`ε = ε_null + 7.5 · (w_lion · d_lion + w_meerkat · d_meerkat)`, with `w_lion + w_meerkat = 2`

It targets the lion × meerkat failure, where the meerkat expert takes over both bodies. No later file adopts it, and the binding route's seven proposed rows do not include it. Three things must be settled before it runs. These come from reading the code and the algebra while writing this note, and nothing has been rendered.

- **The sign flips.** CO3 computes `a = exp(−β · distance)` and sets `w = −a / Σa`, so its weights are negative and subtract. The concept nearest the joint prediction is subtracted most. Copying `exp(−β · distance)` into PoE's positive weights would boost the concept already winning, so the adaptation needs the opposite direction, for example `w_i = 2 · exp(+β · dist_i) / Σ_j exp(+β · dist_j)`.
- **CO3's distance reads the joint prompt.** Line 187 of the corrector measures each concept against `noise_pred_multi`, the joint prompt's prediction. A joint-free version needs another reference. The plain average of the two predictions cannot serve, since both concepts are then exactly `‖ε_A − ε_B‖ / 2` away and the weights never move. The guided PoE prediction gives two different distances whenever `d_A` and `d_B` differ in size.
- **The scale is unmeasured.** The distances are unnormalised norms over the whole latent. At β = 0.9, a gap of 5 between them makes one weight about 90 times the other. The function's `w_min_mag` and `w_max_mag` clipping arguments are never applied, so a joint-free version needs its own limit.

If the user says yes, add it as a method in the binding route with pass marks written first. That route's keep rule is a scorer count on lion × meerkat seeds 3401 to 3432 at least 4 above the kept recipe's, with cat × dog at 33 or more of 128.

**B. Redraw the start several times at one noise level (judged worth less).** CO3 takes its guess at the finished picture and adds noise back, repeating 10, 10, 5, 5, 5 and 5 times over its six steps. The session ranked this low because separation is already 121 of 128. The nearest things tried did not add clean pairs: self-recurrence at cap 0.1, and three repeats per step on the early recipe, which added damage.

**C. CO3's main correction (not usable).** It is the joint prediction minus the average of the single-concept predictions. Without the joint prompt there is nothing to correct from, and avoiding that input is the point of this method.

**D. CO3 pictures as clean training positives (partly done already).** The session offered this as a new idea and warned that the plain studio backdrop could teach the checker "plain backdrop means clean". The kept checker's training pool already holds 342 CO3 pictures from the benchmark pairs (157 clean, 185 rejects). What is untried is a large batch of new CO3 pictures with a check on backdrop style first.
