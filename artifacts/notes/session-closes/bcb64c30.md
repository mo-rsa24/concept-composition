## What it set out to do

This session ran from 2026-11-18 04:19 to 2026-11-19 04:02 UTC, inside `4-inclusive-captions/`. It asked whether a diffusion model composes two digits better when pictures holding both digits are also trained under each digit's own label.

It first added an MNIST stage to [the inclusive-captions route](../../../4-inclusive-captions/plans/route-inclusive-captions-pilot.md#4--ask-the-same-question-on-mnist-digits), settled round by round with the user. It then walked [the MNIST stage plan](../../../4-inclusive-captions/plans/11-do-inclusive-captions-help-poe-compose/plans/02-the-mnist-stage.md) under a loop: 15 small models trained on the laptop, drawn, scored and compared with a bar written before any model trained.

The user also asked side questions about chest X-rays, which the last part of "What it found" records.

**Words this uses**

The **product** adds the noise predictions of two labels, so one picture is pushed to satisfy both. **Corrected** means the product with 20 extra Langevin steps at each noise level. The **dial** is how often a two-digit training canvas is also trained under each single label (0, 0.1, 0.3, 0.6 or 1). A **training** is one of three models at a dial, each from a different seed.

A **separate pair** is a tile showing a right 7 and a right 8 apart. The **judge** is a small digit classifier that calls each tile. An **eye call** is a person or Claude calling a tile by looking at it. A dial **counts** only if "7" alone and "8" alone each draw their own digit alone at least a set number of times, called the **purity floor**, in all three trainings.
