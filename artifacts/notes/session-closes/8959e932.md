## What it set out to do

The session began on 18 November as route step 7b: on MNIST digit canvases, can a small checker's gradient turn one fused digit into two separate digits, and how good must that checker be? The user then asked whether the same repair works on two digit models trained apart (plan 32), and how to compose such models at inference (plan 33).

The goal stated for plan 33 is the full-canvas product of a 0 to 4 expert and a 5 to 9 expert, repaired by classifier guidance and a corrector, with no cropping, on 90 of 100 clean pairs no expert has seen. The user ruled out cropping because a CLEVR scene or a cat and a dog cannot be cut into known regions.

From 20 November the session asked what could be published. On 21 November it planned [the two-paper route](../../../plans/route-two-papers.md), built the progress board and started three looping sessions to save and tidy the project.

| Word | What it means here |
|---|---|
| Canvas | A 32 × 64 pixel MNIST image with room for a digit in each half |
| Clean pair | The judge's call "two clear, right, apart": exactly two blobs, one in each half, read as the asked digits at 0.9 confidence or more |
| The judge | The third digit classifier built in step 7b, which reads each blob of a finished canvas |
| Push | The gradient of a small trained checker, added to the noise prediction at each sampler step |
| Both pushes | The pair-aware checker (is this the asked pair, drawn as two clear digits?) plus the separate-label checker (is digit a present? is digit b present?) |
| Tuning pairs | 1 & 9, 2 & 8, 3 & 6, 4 & 7, 0 & 5, at seeds 0 to 63, so 320 canvases |
| Final pairs | The other 20 pairings of a 0 to 4 digit with a 5 to 9 digit, 64 seeds each, so 1,280 canvases |
| Crop composition | Each expert denoises only its own half-canvas, and a coin flip picks which half |
| E0e | Plan 33's experiment: each expert sees the canvas with the other half replaced by noised blank, the side is chosen from the experts' own label-added prediction differences over the noisy first fifth of steps (t at least 800 of 1000), then held |

## What it found

**One model that saw a few pairs (plan 24, step 7b).** On the model trained with 1% two-digit canvases, both pushes gave 285 of 320 clean pairs (89%) by eye on five held-out pairs at fresh seeds 64 to 127. The judge counts the same canvases at 275. No push gives 1 of 320 by the judge, and the pair-aware push alone gives 215 by eye.

**Two experts trained apart (plan 32).** With each digit free to take either side, the plain product gives 0 of 320 clean pairs and both pushes give 4, against a bar of 224. The canvases are saturated blobs. When each expert always draws on its own fixed side, the product gives 139 of 320 and both pushes give 168, and that control misses its bar of 256.

**Composing them at inference (plan 33).** Clean pairs of 320 on the tuning pairs, by the judge, using plan 32's either-side experts:

| What ran | No push | Both pushes |
|---|---|---|
| Full-canvas product | 0 | 1 |
| Crop composition, coin-flip side | 124 | 257 (298 with a 5-step Langevin corrector) |
| Side chosen at every step, nothing hidden (E0d-a) | 0 | 0 |
| Side chosen at every step, other half hidden (E0d-b) | 34 | 56 |
| Side chosen early and held, other half hidden (E0e) | 259 | 310 |

**The E0e final read.** On the 20 final pairs at fresh seeds 128 to 191, the judge counts **1,200 of 1,280 clean pairs (94%)** with both pushes and 1,043 (81%) without them. The weakest pairs are 3-8 at 56 of 64 and 4-9 at 57 of 64. The written bar is 1,152 of 1,280 and at least 45 of 64 on every pair, which the judge's count meets. The plan's headline is counted by eye, and that count is still owed, so the headline is unclaimed.

**What E0e does not show.** Hiding the other half of a fixed left and right split carries the same information as a crop, so E0e does not meet the no-cropping goal. The review file calls it "composition over two known halves with an inferred side, not a free-form layout". Nothing has tested whether the inferred side beats a coin flip on the real experts.

**The older crop read, for reference.** The crop composition with both pushes and the corrector, on the same 20 pairs at seeds 64 to 127, gives 1,088 of 1,280 (85%) by the judge and 1,136 to about 1,190 (89% to 93%) by eye. The 90% bar sits inside that range, so it was neither met nor missed.

**An exact toy cuts against two of the session's ideas.** The toy has two cells, two experts with exact scores, 50 steps and 4,000 draws per setting. The plain product puts a 4 and a 7 apart on 811 draws. Adding the interaction term (what the product drops from the true joint score) on steps 0 to 9 gives 816, and on steps 10 to 49 gives 4,000. The term is 0.05% of the total score over steps 0 to 9, and a draw is 90% decided by step 29, so in this toy the term acts late. A held coin-flip layout gives 3,859 against 3,840 for the voted layout, so the vote adds nothing here. A toy with exact scores cannot settle what trained networks do.

**Novelty is unsettled.** Eight scoped searches found the ingredients in prior work and no paper doing the whole combination. The session judged E0e close to MultiDiffusion with a blank context. `/pressure-test` was never run.
