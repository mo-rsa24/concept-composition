### Words this note uses

**Faithful pair.** One whole cat beside one whole dog, both photographic.

**Two bodies.** Two separate animals in the frame, whatever species each one became.

**The correction.** The joint prompt's noise prediction minus PoE's, `eps_joint - eps_PoE`, at one step. It is the interaction term PoE drops, scaled by the noise level.

**Compose rate.** The fraction of renders showing two separate animals.

**The push.** A small network's gradient added to PoE's prediction at each step, with SDXL frozen.
