## What it set out to do

Session `49d5b78b-8354-4940-955c-b72c3dc1006b` ran from 2026-11-18 14:31 UTC to 2026-11-19 10:26 UTC (16:31 to 12:26 local) in `4-inclusive-captions`, on branch `exec/classifier-guidance-ladder`.

The project asks one question. If a training picture showing two things is also filed under each thing's single caption, does the product of those two captions draw both things? The session tested this on a toy of red circles and blue squares and on MNIST digits.

It began with "where are the toy results?" and grew through five jobs, in this order:

1. Copy the toy relabelling results from `3-understanding-compositionality` into this project and explain them with figures precise enough to survive a supervisor's questions.
2. Answer "is adding pictures unfair?" by rerunning the toy with pictures replaced, so the training set keeps its size.
3. Draw the MNIST stage's figures and rename its "dial" setting to "share".
4. Rerun MNIST with the share set directly at round values, then redraw it at guidance 1.
5. Build a supervisor-meeting deck of 12 slides with a talk-through.

**Words this uses**

**Share**: of the training pictures under one single caption, the percentage that show both things. **The product**: the sampler's combined noise estimate, ε_blank + g·[(ε_a − ε_blank) + (ε_b − ε_blank)]. **Guidance (g)**: the multiplier in that formula, 3 by default; at 1 the formula is the exact product p(x | a) p(x | b) / p(x). **Corrector**: 5 or 20 extra Langevin steps at each noise level. **Clean pair**: a finished picture with both things apart, called by a colour scorer on the toy ("red and blue apart") and by a pinned CNN judge on MNIST ("two separate right digits"). **Adding** puts extra both-shapes pictures under a caption, so the set grows. **Replacing** swaps some of a caption's pictures for both-shapes ones, so the set stays at 6,000. **A training**: one model from one seed. Each setting has three.
