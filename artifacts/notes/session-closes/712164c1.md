# Session close: the route for binding each body to its concept

written from the transcript

## What it set out to do

Session `712164c1` ran on 2026-11-19 from 02:34 to 02:42 UTC, in `2-classifier-guidance` on branch `exec/classifier-guidance-ladder`. It started `/drip-skill-route --research` to plan, one decision per round, how to make classifier-guided product-of-experts sampling work on pairs it never saw.

The readout checker's push already makes two separate bodies, but it does not decide which body becomes which concept, so lion × meerkat ends as two meerkats. The route was to test three ways of adding that decision, cheapest first:

1. Regional binding: run each concept's expert only inside its own region.
2. A cross-attention term in the push: each concept word's map peaks on its own body, and the two maps overlap less.
3. Two checker changes taken from MNIST: feed the asked pair to every layer of the checker's head, and retrain the checker twice on its own pushed pictures.

Each method would be judged on cat × dog seeds 6001 to 6128, lion × meerkat seeds 3401 to 3432 and typewriter × cactus seeds 3301 to 3316, with pass marks written before its render. The session planned only. It rendered nothing, trained nothing, launched nothing and changed no code.

### Words this note uses

**Product of experts (PoE):** SDXL sampling with the noise prediction `eps_cat + eps_dog - eps_null`, guidance 7.5, 50 DDIM steps. It tends to fuse the two concepts into one animal.

**Readout checker and push:** the checker is a small classifier that reads SDXL's internal features. The push is its gradient, added to sampling.

**The kept recipe:** the checker `readout_pair/readout-multipair/head_step_002000.pt` at strength 30, each step's push capped at 0.4 of the product prediction's norm, on steps 0 to 15, then plain PoE.

**Faithful pair and two bodies:** verdicts from the automatic scorer `score_sheet.py`. A faithful pair is one clear instance of each concept. Two bodies means two separate things are in the picture, whichever they are.

**Clean or near-clean:** a call made by eye on a full-size tile.

**Oracle layout:** boxes taken from the joint prompt's own render of the same seed, which is the best layout any method could find.
