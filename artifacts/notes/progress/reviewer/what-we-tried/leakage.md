# What we tried on leakage: Paper 2 on CO3, and the detours into coloured digits and the LoRA

This group covers Loop 12: where, in SDXL sampled with CO3 (the released composition sampler that corrects steps 0 to 5 of 50), one animal borrows the other's parts, what predicts it, and what stops it, plus the loop's two detours (finished experts on Colored MNIST, and the pooled LoRA on new animal pairs) and the LoRA plus boxes test from the binding plan. It holds 17 finished attempts, in the order they ran; the literature read that opened the loop ([the mechanism table](../../leakage/01-reading/mechanism-table.md), 13 papers) is a reading, so it has no entry. Words used throughout: **two clear** is a picture with exactly one clear body of each named animal, neither carrying the other's parts, called blind by two reviewers with a third settling splits on [Loop 6's rubric](../../method-search/01-beat-the-baseline/blind-count-rubric.md) with drawing style ignored; **borrowed** is W2d's hand label for two separate subjects where one carries a clear part of the other ([the part-binding calls](../../noise-text-critic/02d-binding/part-binding-calls.csv)); **the six borrowing pairs** are cheetah × cougar, duck × goose, puma × condor, boar × pheasant, bobcat × quail and seal × gull at seeds 6001 to 6008, 48 pictures a row; a **lead** is how many more two-clear pictures one row has than another on the same seeds, and its **McNemar p** is the exact one-sided test on those matched seeds; a **grey level** is one step of 255 in a pixel's value, and "moved by 2.3 grey levels" means the mean absolute pixel difference between two pictures of the same seed.

### Which of three readings inside SDXL predicts which pairs borrow (Z2)

- **Asked:** Does the similarity of the two nouns in the text encoder, the overlap of their attention maps, or the similarity of their pushes on the picture predict which of CO3's pairs borrow parts?
- **Paper:** 2.
- **Tried:** CO3 re-rendered on the 12 tuning pairs at seeds 6001 to 6008 (96 pictures, one RTX 3090 card, Slurm job 94591), with one extra U-Net call at 14 of the 50 steps capturing attention and each animal's push; each reading averaged per pair in three windows (early, steps 0 to 5; middle, 10 to 20; late, 25 to 45) and set against W2d's borrowed count (0 to 8 of 8 seeds per pair).
- **Bar, written first:** seven tests; each is support when Spearman ρ (rank correlation over the 12 pairs) is above 0 with a one-sided permutation p below 0.05 / 7 = 0.0071, which on these counts needs ρ of at least 0.706 (`z2_bars.py`, committed before the first run).
- **Result:** the largest ρ is 0.489 (late attention overlap, p 0.056, 95% interval −0.17 to 0.86); text similarity gives ρ 0.137 (p 0.341); the other five run from −0.432 to 0.228. Crow × raven, which borrows on 0 of 8 seeds, reads highest of the 12 pairs on all three readings; puma × condor reads low and borrows on 7 of 8. Within the three pairs that hold both labels, borrowed and clean seeds show no consistent difference (late overlap 0.816 against 0.839, 0.912 against 0.868, 0.578 against 0.574). Of 20 secondary readings with no bar, the largest is the second noun's overlap with the end-of-text token's map, early window, ρ 0.740.
- **Verdict:** null on all seven tests.
- **Why it failed:** the three readings follow how alike the two animals are, and borrowing does not (verified: crow × raven tops every reading and never borrows). On cheetah × cougar at seed 6001 the borrowing looks like an unfinished change of animal: plain SDXL draws two cheetahs and CO3's push away from the cheetah at steps 4 and 5 finishes a cougar-shaped animal with cheetah spots (inferred, one seed, [the capture note](../../leakage/co3-inside/design.md)). A signal at one step or in one region of the canvas would be averaged away by the window means (inferred).
- **Kind of failure:** the idea, for these three readings averaged per pair over a window: the test was fair (bar fixed in code before the run, the capture changed the picture by 0.0 of 255, every prompt parsed into its two nouns), and crow × raven tops every reading while borrowing on 0 of 8. Test design as well: 12 pairs give 95% intervals wider than one unit of ρ, so a modest relationship is not excluded, and a window mean would hide a signal at one step or one region.
- **Idea still alive?** closed for the three window-averaged readings as predictors of which pairs borrow. Unclear for a reading taken at one step or in one region, and for the end-of-text overlap (ρ 0.740, one of 20 looked at); a test of that one reading on new pairs with its bar written first would tell.
- **Rules out:** Do not aim a fix at keeping similar subjects apart (Bounded Attention, Conform) on the claim that similarity causes borrowing, and do not reuse these three per-pair readings as a predictor of borrowing.
- **Leaves open:** whether a reading at a single step or region predicts it; the end-of-text overlap (ρ 0.740, the largest of 20 looked at, so possibly chance) is untested on new pairs; with 12 pairs every 95% interval spans more than one unit of ρ, so a modest relationship is not ruled out. The reviewer agrees with the null ("pairs that look alike borrow parts: wrong as stated") and marks [the what-and-when picture](../../leakage/02-what-predicts-borrowing/z2-what-and-when.png) as overclaiming ("earliest window that passes = when borrowing is set"), since a correlation across pairs says when borrowing becomes readable and no window passed ([the reviewer's Loop 12 section](../2026-11-24-review.md#loop-12-where-co3-leaks)).
- **Evidence:** [Z2's review](../../leakage/02-what-predicts-borrowing/review.md), [its design](../../leakage/02-what-predicts-borrowing/design.md), [the two-pair small read](../../leakage/02-what-predicts-borrowing/small-read/README.md); W&B `llct9xp0` (full), `27u2m8sh` (small read), `lmm75rgv` and `pn7tsanv` (smoke) (board step Z2).

### Whether a per-place signal finds the borrowing body, read from saved records (Z3's free check)

- **Asked:** From records that already exist, does a per-cell map of where each animal's push acts find the pairs that borrow, and does CO3's own signal read cell by cell find the body that borrows?
- **Paper:** 2.
- **Tried:** no GPU. Z2's per-cell push-length cosine between the two animals, per window, over the 12 pairs; and, from the CO3 capture run (Slurm job 94450, seed 6001 only), the per-cell difference between each animal's distance to the joint prompt's prediction, on cheetah × cougar and eagle × fox at steps 0 to 5.
- **Bar, written first:** none recorded; it was decided on board question 91 as a look before the drawn-box test.
- **Result:** the push-length cosine gives ρ 0.421 early, 0.171 middle and −0.068 late against the borrowed count, with crow × raven again highest in every window (cosines 0.97, 0.93, 0.91). On eagle × fox each body reads closer to its own animal by steps 4 and 5 (the eagle closer on 62% of all cells at step 5); on cheetah × cougar both bodies read closer to the cheetah (80% of all cells at step 5).
- **Verdict:** null for finding the pairs; the per-place read is one seed per pair and carries no verdict.
- **Why it failed:** CO3's per-cell signal marks the borrowing body as "cheetah" and the cheetah's own body as "cheetah" too, so it cannot say which body should be which (verified on one seed); CO3's rule applied per cell (push harder away from the closer animal) would push the cheetah off its own body as well (inferred from that map).
- **Kind of failure:** the idea, for CO3's per-cell signal as a guide: on the recorded seed it reads "cheetah" on the cheetah's body and on the borrowing body alike, so it cannot say which body should be which. Test design: one seed per pair, because the capture run recorded only seed 6001.
- **Idea still alive?** closed as a fix on its own (it needs a layout from elsewhere, and Paper 2's method file closes per-cell CO3 weights). Unclear whether the per-cell pattern holds beyond one seed; a capture run on other seeds of the six borrowing pairs would tell.
- **Rules out:** Do not apply CO3's own per-cell weights as a fix; a per-place correction needs a layout from somewhere else.
- **Leaves open:** whether the per-cell pattern holds on other seeds; the capture recorded seed 6001 only, so a new run is needed.
- **Evidence:** [the free check](../../leakage/03-one-fix/free-check/README.md) with [its per-place maps](../../leakage/03-one-fix/free-check/per-place-distance-maps.png); Slurm job 94450; W&B `llct9xp0` for the push-length cosines (board step Z3).

### Subtracting the other animal inside hand-drawn boxes, late in sampling (Z3)

- **Asked:** With each animal's region known exactly, does correcting each region against the other animal's prediction at steps 15 to 30 give more pictures with two clear subjects than CO3 alone?
- **Paper:** 2.
- **Tried:** the six borrowing pairs, three rows of 48: CO3 alone (Z2's renders), one extra CO3 corrector pass per step at steps 15 to 30 with CO3's whole-picture weights, and the same pass with, inside each animal's box, twice the joint prompt's guess minus the other animal's guess (the animal's own prediction at weight 0). Boxes drawn by eye on CO3's finished pictures before any fix render; 96 new renders (Slurm job 94678), about 1.6 GPU-hours; 144 pictures called blind.
- **Bar, written first:** support when the drawn-box row leads CO3 alone by at least 4 with McNemar p below 0.05 (in practice a lead of 5 with no seed lost), its broken count is at most CO3 alone's plus 3, and the whole-picture row does not also pass (`z3_bars.py`).
- **Result:** two clear 5 of 48 in every row; broken 1 (CO3 alone), 2 (whole picture), 4 (drawn box). Drawn box against CO3 alone: 3 seeds gained, 3 lost, p 0.66. Reviewers agreed on 128 of 144 pictures (89%). In the reviewers' notes on cheetah × cougar, cheetah spots on the cougar fall from 6 and 7 mentions (CO3 alone, reviewers 1 and 2) to 2 and 2 (drawn box), while cheetah tear lines on its face rise from 2 and 3 to 6 and 5; duck × goose keeps mallard heads on goose bodies in all 24 pictures.
- **Verdict:** null.
- **Why it failed:** the rule never put an animal's own prediction in its box, so the joint prediction, which carries the cheetah's features, stayed the content there (verified from `z3_regions.py` lines 66 to 72). The correction moved texture and left structure: spots left the cougar's body, and the tear lines, the mallard heads and the condor's puma head stayed (verified from the notes and the blind calls). Structure may be set before step 15, since the borrowing body reads "cheetah" by steps 4 and 5 on the recorded seed (inferred; the reviewer calls its own earlier advice to wait until step 15 "partly wrong"). The all-or-nothing count also fails pictures for extra, missing or doubled subjects, which 13 of CO3 alone's 43 failures carry (inferred from the reviewer's keyword count).
- **Kind of failure:** not understood: the test was read as a test of regions while the content inside each box stayed the joint prediction, which carries the cheetah's features (`z3_regions.py` lines 66 to 72), and its window of steps 15 to 30 came from advice the recorded seed contradicts (the borrowing body reads "cheetah" by steps 4 and 5). Test design: 48 pictures with an all-or-nothing count, where a pass needs a lead of 5 with no seed lost, and the failure rung also takes pictures with extra or missing subjects.
- **Idea still alive?** unclear for subtracting the other animal inside its region: steps 6 to 14, more than one pass per step, and removing only the other animal's component (Concept Algebra's projection, about 0.8 GPU-hours) are untested, and any of them run against a whole-picture pass in the same window would tell. Regions as such stay open: Z7 put the own prediction in the same boxes.
- **Rules out:** Do not repeat "twice the joint guess minus the other animal" inside boxes at steps 15 to 30 with one pass per step, and do not read this null as showing that regions cannot work: the review and the reviewer both say it tested only subtraction.
- **Leaves open:** steps 6 to 14 are untested; more than one pass per step is untested; removing only the other animal's component (Concept Algebra's projection, about 0.8 GPU-hours in [the method review](../../leakage/method-review.md)) is untested. The manager's table records two disagreeing counts of what CO3 draws: two separate bodies in 96 of 96 tuning pictures (Loop 6) against 23 of 96 labelled "not two" (Loop 9); if Loop 9's labels hold, about a third of CO3's failures are extra subjects no borrowed-parts fix can repair ([things that may be wrong](../../manager/status.md#things-that-may-be-wrong)). Not settled.
- **Evidence:** [Z3's review](../../leakage/03-one-fix/review.md), [its design](../../leakage/03-one-fix/design.md), [the boxes](../../leakage/03-one-fix/boxes.json), [the smoke picture](../../leakage/03-one-fix/smoke/smoke-three-rows.png), [the small read](../../leakage/03-one-fix/small/README.md); W&B `xql3iwpp` (full), `x1gpc3pz` (small), `p3ydtazf` (smoke); [the reviewer's second Loop 12 pass](../2026-11-24-review.md#loop-12-again-at-2300-two-nulls-in-a-row) (board step Z3).

Unclear for regions read from each animal's push: Z3 never tested a rule that uses the own prediction, and a rule with those regions and the own prediction inside them, against the same rule with hand boxes, would tell. - **Rules out:** Do not reopen the four closed candidates without naming new evidence against the reason each was closed. Do not offer a diffusion-trained method while a training-free one is untested.
- **Leaves open:** the text-side swap is untested and cannot reach borrowing by the first-named animal (boar × pheasant); Bounded Attention has not run; the identity reward LoRA is held. **Two records disagree on what was chosen:** the board's Z4 note says "chosen to test next: the text-side token swap (after a word-order check), beside Bounded Attention as the rival", while the method file, the verdict and the method review name each animal's own prediction in its box as next and hold the text-side fix. **The reviewer and the closure of regions disagree:** the method file closes regions read from each push "as a fix" on the strength of Z3's null, and the reviewer marks "the null shows regions cannot work as a fix" as wrong as stated, because Z3 never used the animal's own prediction. **Two records disagree on the push-region read:** the method file says it is "not to be spent", and board question 97 proposed spending it; the reviewer reports both. None of the three is resolved here.
- **Evidence:** [Paper 2's method](../../leakage/04-paper-2-method.md), [the verdict](../../leakage/06-verdict.md), [the method review](../../leakage/method-review.md), [the reviewer's second Loop 12 pass](../2026-11-24-review.md#loop-12-again-at-2300-two-nulls-in-a-row) (board steps Z4 and Z6).
