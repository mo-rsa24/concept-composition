# LoRA correction

**The question:** can a small LoRA adapter, trained on the correction the product of experts drops,
make SDXL's product draw two separate animals, and does it carry to pairs it never saw?

This is the ICLR paper's project: scopes 01 to 09 in `plans/`, their findings in `report/`,
and their figures, walks and idea maps in `artifacts/`. Context, environment, runbook and the
`poe_repair` package are shared at the repo root.
