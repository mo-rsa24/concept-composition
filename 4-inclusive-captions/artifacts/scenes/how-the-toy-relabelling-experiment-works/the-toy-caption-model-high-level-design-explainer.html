Read it top to bottom. Pictures with caption ids train a VAE that squeezes each picture into 128 numbers; a fully connected denoiser learns to guess the clean 128 numbers from a noisy copy and a caption id; and sampling calls that one denoiser three times per step and adds the answers up as the product of the two single captions. Every picture on the diagram is real: drawn by the experiment's code, sampled by its saved runs, or recorded from one retrained model, as each card says.

Where each box comes from, and what is not drawn

Where each box comes from

All code paths are under 3-understanding-compositionality/artifacts/notes/what-poe-loses-when-it-composes/, at commit db1325d7; every number is read from toy_relabelling_numbers.json beside this page, written by make_toy_relabelling_numbers.py.

The three piles of pictures: toy_sampler_hybrids.py draw, CIRCLES, SQUARES (16 x 32 x 3 pixels, a circle of 44 pixels or a square of 36); toy_caption_dial_ldm.py PAIRS (the 2 both-shapes layouts). 2,000 per caption.

Relabel k pictures: corpus_k in toy_caption_threshold.py. Exactly k of the 2,000 both-shapes pictures are added again under id 0 and again under id 1, so the set grows from 6,000 to 6,000 + 2k pictures. Counts run: 0, 1, 5, 20, 50, 200, 600, 1,200, 2,000; three trainings each.

VAE: class VAE in toy_caption_dial_ldm.py, 45,707 parameters, latent 4 x 4 x 8 = 128 numbers. Trained 4,000 steps, batch 256, learning rate 0.001, KL weight 0.0001; restarted up to 5 times until reconstruction error is at most 0.012.

Add noise at level t and Squared error: train_denoiser. z_t = sqrt(abar_t) z0 + sqrt(1 − abar_t) noise, cosine schedule with offset 0.008 over 200 levels. The loss compares the output with the clean latent, so the denoiser guesses z0, never the noise. 8,000 steps, batch 256, learning rate 0.0002.

Caption id: ids 0 'a red circle', 1 'a blue square', 2 'a red circle and a blue square', 3 'empty'. During training the id is replaced by 3, the empty caption, on 10% of draws (train_denoiser), which is what makes guidance possible.

Caption vector: nn.Embedding, a learned 4 x 64 table. No text is read anywhere in the model.

Denoiser: class Denoiser, fully connected, 3 hidden layers of 1,024 with SiLU. Input: 128 noisy latent numbers, 64 time features and the 64-number caption row, 256 in all; 128 out. 2,493,824 parameters, caption table included.

3 denoiser calls and the product: draw. Each call's guess z0_hat becomes a noise estimate, e = (z_t - sqrt(abar_t) * z0_hat) / sqrt(1 - abar_t); the product is e = e_empty + g * [(e_circle - e_empty) + (e_square - e_empty)] with g = 3; then one ancestral step.

Langevin corrector: toy_caption_corrector.py. After each step: z <- z + eta * s + sqrt(2 eta) * noise, s = -e / sqrt(1 - abar_j), eta = 0.05 * (1 - abar_j). 5 moves per level is the main setting, 20 the stronger one.

Count the shapes: toy_blob_scorer.py read. Each pixel goes to the nearest of white, red, blue and purple, or to none when further than 0.35. Categories: red and blue apart; red only; blue only; purple or overlaid; nothing readable. "red and blue apart" means at least 12 red and 12 blue pixels, centres at least 6 pixels apart in x, and fewer than 12 purple pixels. A count such as 0 of 300 is how many of a model's 300 product samples fall in that category.

How to read the lines and colours

Solid arrows carry data the next part waits on. The dotted two-headed arrow is the Langevin corrector, which runs only when switched on. The denoiser in lane 4 is the one trained in lane 3, called three times per step.

Outline colours follow the ladder's key: blue for inputs, green for the hidden parts, red for what a network puts out, orange for the method's own parts (the product and the corrector), grey for a frozen part. Colour never fills a shape, because the toy's own pictures are red circles and blue squares.

Grey squares in a latent picture are its numbers, black for low and white for high, laid out as its 4 channels of 4 x 8.

What is not drawn

The one-caption sampler (e = e_empty + 3 (e_caption − e_empty)) is the same loop with two calls; it is described here, not drawn.

The older relabelling by a random share (corpus, the share from 0 to 1) appears only in the corrector pictures, which come from those models; the counts experiment uses exact counts k.

The template-library scorer in toy_sampler_hybrids.py is not what the counts use, so only the colour scorer is drawn.

The VAE's KL term and log-variance clamp sit inside the VAE boxes and are not drawn as parts.
