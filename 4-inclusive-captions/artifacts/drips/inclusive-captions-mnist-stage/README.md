# The inclusive-captions question on MNIST digits

This walk trained 15 small digit models, three at each of five settings for how often a two-digit canvas is also trained under each single label, and tested whether the product of two labels draws two separate digits. The trainings finished on 2026-11-19. The verdict recorded on 2026-11-22 is null, because the settings that count draw at most a mean of 8.0 separate right pairs of 64 against a null line of 9.75.
