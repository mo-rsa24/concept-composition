# MNIST digits on a canvas: how good must the checker be for its push to compose two digits

The walk trained digit models on two-digit MNIST canvases and tested whether the push from a checker told the asked pair turns one fused digit into two. Counted by eye on the five digit pairs the model never saw together, the pair-aware push alone gave two clear, right digits on 215 of 320 fresh seeds, and adding the separate-label push gave 285 of 320.
