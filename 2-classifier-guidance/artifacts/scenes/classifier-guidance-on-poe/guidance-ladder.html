classifier guidance · chosen

Learns: is this becoming a clean cat and dog?

It needs no promise about PoE's maths, only good labels. The push moves toward pictures that score higher on that one question. This is what worked on the circle-and-square toy.

zoom 1One drawing step, 50 times over

The three U-Nets are the same frozen SDXL, run with three prompts. Adding the cat and dog predictions makes both experts pull toward one shared body, which is where the fused cat-dog comes from. The push is the only new part.

zoom 2Inside the checker

Only the amber part learns. SDXL already knows what cats and dogs look like at every noise level, so the head only has to learn which pattern of those features means "two separate animals, the right two".

zoom 3How it learns to penalise hybrids, and what the push does

The checker is never told a rule. Seeing hybrids labelled 0 is enough: hybrid-looking states get low scores, so the gradient at a hybrid points away from it, toward what clean pairs look like at that step. The random push of the same size shows the change comes from that direction and not from shaking the drawing.

Where these numbers come from, and what they cannot show
  • Sampler: 50 DDIM steps, classifier-free guidance 7.5 on each expert, PoE as ε_cat + ε_dog − ε_null (poe_repair/_sdxl/metrics.py); push cap 0.1 (poe_repair/methods/_classifier_guided.py).
  • Checker: scripts/rung2/train_readout_pair.py, PairHead reading train_count_head.Readout; the weight count is summed from its layer shapes.
  • Accuracy is balanced accuracy, clean pair against reject, averaged over the first 20 sampler steps, on eval_set_catdog_v2.json (109 clean, 111 rejects, never trained on). Mid-run readings use 5 of the 20 steps, so they are not strictly comparable to final ones.
  • Push calls are draft calls from full-size tiles on held-out seeds 9 to 16; the user's own calls are the ones that count.
  • The discriminator figures are the read map's 1D test (mean absolute score error), not an SDXL measurement.
  • CLIP features on finished pictures reach 0.771 on the same question, which suggests a ceiling for whole-picture checkers near there; looking at each animal separately is the planned route past it.
