# Retrospective: classifier guidance reproduced on the released weights (route step 4)

The walk reproduced Dhariwal and Nichol's classifier guidance (arXiv 2105.05233) on their released 256 px unconditional ImageNet model and classifier, in four targets, each against a bar written to [route step 4](../../../plans/route-classifier-guided-poe-from-toy-to-animals.md) before it ran. All four passed. The results are on [the reproduce card](../../results/does-classifier-guidance-work-on-the-released-weights/README.md).

## T1: scale 0 against plain sampling

**The request.** "Check that classifier scale 0 gives a render byte-identical to plain sampling, on 4 seeds."

**The problem.** The product sampler will carry the same push, so a push that changes the image at strength 0 would contaminate every later comparison. The repository's sampler also draws random class labels before sampling, which shifts the noise against plain sampling, and co3 lacked mpi4py and blobfile, which the repository's `dist_util` imports.

**What was done.** Before the run: the repository was cloned on the laptop and on the cluster at commit `22e0df8`, and the weights were downloaded to `/datasets`, one file at a time after the server reset parallel downloads near 800 MB. A wrapper was written that imports `script_util` and never `dist_util`, seeds right before sampling, and samples each seed as its own batch of 1. Plain sampling and scale 0 ran on seeds 0 to 3, and plain sampling ran a second time on seed 0 as the determinism control (Slurm job 89232, mscluster108).

**Where the results live.** <a href="../../results/does-classifier-guidance-work-on-the-released-weights/sheet-identity-scale-0-against-plain.png"><img src="../../results/does-classifier-guidance-work-on-the-released-weights/sheet-identity-scale-0-against-plain.png" width="200" alt="identity sheet, plain sampling over scale 0 on four seeds"></a> and `identity/identity_checksums.csv` beside it.

**Before and after.** Before: whether scale 0 equals plain sampling was stated by the README and untested. After: sha256 equal on 4 of 4 seeds, on the uint8 render and on the float32 sample, with the control equal too.

**Links to.** This is the first target, and the three below rest on the same wrapper and seeds.

## T2: the corgi figure

**The request.** "Reproduce the paper's corgi figure: the unconditional model pushed to 'Pembroke Welsh corgi' at classifier scale 1.0 and 10.0, 16 seeds each."

**The problem.** The paper's Figure 3 shows the claim only as pictures and an FID per scale, and the FID needs 50,000 samples.

**What was done.** Seeds 0 to 15 were rendered at scales 1 and 10 (Slurm job 89235), and the released classifier's top-1 call was recorded at t = 0 on each render. Seed 0's raw gradient size and applied push size were logged at every step.

**Where the results live.** <a href="../../results/does-classifier-guidance-work-on-the-released-weights/sheet-pembroke-welsh-corgi-scale-1-and-10.png"><img src="../../results/does-classifier-guidance-work-on-the-released-weights/sheet-pembroke-welsh-corgi-scale-1-and-10.png" width="200" alt="corgi sheet, scale 1 against scale 10"></a>, `guide/classifier_calls_263.csv` and `guide/push_per_step_c263_s*_seed0.json`.

**Before and after.** Scale 1: 2 of 16 called Pembroke, mean probability 0.067. Scale 10: 16 of 16, mean 0.854; by eye 13 of 16 clear corgis. The push at scale 10 is under 0.1% of the step's mean norm (0.32 against about 429 at step 50 of 250).

**Links to.** It uses T1's wrapper, now with the push switched on.

## T3: tabby cat

**The request.** "Run the same at scale 1.0 and 10.0 for one cat class (tabby) and one dog class (golden retriever), 16 seeds each."

**The problem.** This project's experiments need cat and dog, which the paper never shows.

**What was done.** Tabby (ImageNet 281) and golden retriever ran as one process. The Slurm job 89245 sat pending 30 minutes because another user took mscluster108, so it was cancelled and the same script ran with nohup on mscluster107's free card 1.

**Where the results live.** <a href="../../results/does-classifier-guidance-work-on-the-released-weights/sheet-tabby-cat-scale-1-and-10.png"><img src="../../results/does-classifier-guidance-work-on-the-released-weights/sheet-tabby-cat-scale-1-and-10.png" width="200" alt="tabby sheet, scale 1 against scale 10"></a> and `guide/classifier_calls_281_207.csv`.

**Before and after.** Scale 1: 2 of 16, mean 0.066. Scale 10: 15 of 16, mean 0.737. The miss, seed 8, is an ornate object called pickelhaube. By eye 12 of 16 clear tabbies.

**Links to.** It is T2's run with a different class.
